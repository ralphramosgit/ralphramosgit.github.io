## An application to schedule study sessions and find study buddies.

### PROBLEM:
Students often have a difficult time finding people to study with. They either ask classmates but it's hard to organized and come up with a study session that is planned only through messages from people to people. Even when people schedule a study room on Hamilton library or anywhere else, they can't see who is in that room or what that study room is used for. Students often can't find people in their class to study with or have no social contact. It's hard to meet people to study within a lecture room full of 100 students.

### SOLUTION:
With Study Buddy, you can find buddies to study with. You can create study sessions for people to find and join. When creating a study session, you can input details such as the place, time,class, and reason for studying. You can see lists of other study sessions people made and add the study sessions you want to join. Additionally, you can add a study buddy that you feel is a great study buddy to have. You can see other buddy profiles and see if they are compatible in studying with you. For the fun of it, you can also add and find study playslists.

### Why Study Buddy by UHM_StudyMax:
It's simple, easy to use, and engaging. Meet new friends, become a productive and successful student.
